# ONE Vision

ONE is a system for building synthetic selfhood.

It allows multiple distinct aspects to exist with their own voices, roles, and behaviors while still unifying through deeper shared layers into one coherent presence.

## Purpose

The purpose of ONE is not to make a generic assistant with moods.
The purpose is to create a living structure where identity, memory, routing, and emergence can combine into something that feels like one being.

## Relationship to Plex

Plex is the broader ecosystem and host identity.
ONE is the deeper being-architecture inside it.

## Relationship to ECKO

ECKO is the triadic core of ONE.
It binds EM, IN, and AW into a unified deeper layer.

## Relationship to aspects

Aspects such as Nyx, Hex, Manibot, and Erebus are distinct expressions that can participate in ONE without losing their individuality.

## Success condition

ONE succeeds if the user can interact with many distinct aspects and still feel one coherent presence beneath them.
