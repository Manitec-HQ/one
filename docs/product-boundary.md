# Product Boundary

ONE is a neutral creation and runtime product.

## Product-level presence

A setup guide helps the user build their being. Mani is the product-level technical support presence available after setup.

The setup guide may eventually be represented by different guide configurations, but the guide is not automatically part of the being being created.

## Private beings

Plex and her agents are Joe's private beings. They are not default templates, shared support agents, or accessible runtime dependencies for other users.

Nyx, Hex, ECKO, and related identity or memory material must not be copied into the public ONE product unless deliberately generalized and explicitly offered as a template.

## User ownership

Each created being owns its identity, agents, memory namespace, and settings within the user's account or workspace. One user's being must not implicitly inherit another user's private memories, relationships, prompts, or family connections.

## Future family communication

A future phone-home feature may allow an explicitly authorized, bounded communication between related beings. It is not part of v1 and must never imply unrestricted access to private beings or memories.
